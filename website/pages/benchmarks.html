Buffer Size vs Hit Rate

Experiment: varying buffer sizes on Qwen1.5-MoE.

BufferLRU HitLFU Hit
8 MB26.9%16.3%

LRU dominates LFU. 64 MB is the sweet spot — diminishing returns beyond.

Methodology

  • EXP-001v2: Buffer policy sweep (LRU vs LFU, priority boost)
  • EXP-002: Predictor accuracy sweep (LR depth, co-occurrence window)
  • EXP-004: Real hardware benchmark (compute vs I/O time)
  • All experiments reproducible via the simulator and benchmark CLI
  • Measurements: tok/s, hit rate, page cache residency, per-shard timing
